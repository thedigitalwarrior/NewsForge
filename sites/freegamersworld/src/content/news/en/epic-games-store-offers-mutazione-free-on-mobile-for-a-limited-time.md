## A Free Mutant Soap Opera

Epic Games Store's mobile app is currently offering *Mutazione* as a free download for both iOS and Android devices. The giveaway was flagged by the deal-tracking account cagdeals on Threads and confirmed by gg.deals, which reported that the game can be claimed at no cost until August 6.

*Mutazione* is a narrative-driven walking simulator originally developed by Die Gute Fabrik. The story follows Kai, a teenage girl who travels to a small, isolated community populated by mutants after her grandfather suffers a heart attack. While there, she becomes entangled in the daily lives and personal dramas of the town's residents, forming friendships and uncovering secrets tied to a larger, world-spanning mystery. The game blends soap-opera-style storytelling with supernatural elements, and its slower pace focuses on dialogue, exploration, and character relationships rather than combat or action.

## How to Claim It

As with previous Epic mobile giveaways, players need to have the Epic Games Store app installed on their phone or tablet to claim *Mutazione*. Once claimed before the offer expires, the title is added permanently to the user's library, meaning it does not need to be re-downloaded before the promotional window closes to keep it.

This promotion is separate from Epic's long-running weekly PC giveaways, which the storefront has continued to run in parallel. According to gg.deals, Epic has already confirmed the next batch of PC freebies, which will include a mystery-themed book adventure and a cooperative puzzle title, continuing the platform's practice of alternating between mobile and desktop promotions.

## Part of a Broader Push on Mobile

Epic has been steadily expanding its presence on mobile platforms following its legal disputes with Apple and Google over app store policies, which resulted in Epic regaining the ability to distribute its own store and games directly to iOS and Android users in various regions. The regular mobile giveaways, which have featured a mix of indie and mid-sized titles since the program began, appear to be one way the company is trying to build habitual engagement with its mobile storefront, encouraging users to check in regularly rather than treating the app as a one-time install.

Users interested in adding *Mutazione* to their library should do so before the offer window closes on August 6, after which the game is expected to return to its normal paid price on the Epic Games Store mobile app.
