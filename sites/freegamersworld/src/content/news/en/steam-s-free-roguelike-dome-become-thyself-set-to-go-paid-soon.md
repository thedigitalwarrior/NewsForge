Steam players still have a chance to add the roguelike **Dome: Become Thyself** to their libraries at no cost, though the developer has signaled that the free access period is coming to a close.

## What is Dome: Become Thyself

Developed by Nenad Juric, Dome: Become Thyself is currently the only title the developer has published on Steam. The game is set within the "infinite world of Akashic Records," where players wander through procedurally generated rooms and biomes, encounter alternate versions of themselves, and piece together the hidden logic of the game's reality. Its core mechanic revolves around collecting words and stringing them into sentences that grant abilities, forming the backbone of its roguelike progression system.

The game has been offered as a free-to-play title on Steam since the beginning of July 2026, letting anyone try it out without payment.

## Why the free access is ending

In a Steam announcement, the developer stated that the free playtest phase "has done its job" and confirmed plans to switch Dome: Become Thyself to a paid model. As part of that transition, the free version will be pulled from the storefront, after which new users will no longer be able to add the game to their accounts without buying it.

The developer did not give a specific date for when the change will take effect, saying only that it would happen "shortly." This vague timeline does not necessarily mean a full 1.0 release is imminent — it simply means the free listing could disappear at any time, so players interested in grabbing it should not wait.

## How to claim it, and what makes this different

Unlike Steam's regular "Free to Keep" promotions, which rotate a single complimentary title through the store for a limited time, Dome: Become Thyself has been available under a standard free-to-play model throughout this period. That distinction matters: anyone who adds the game to their library before the switch will keep permanent access to it even after it becomes a paid product, regardless of whether they choose to install or play it right away. Simply clicking "Add to Library" is enough to secure it.

## The broader picture

Despite PC gaming's reputation for being a costly hobby, opportunities like this are common. Steam regularly features free games players can claim, and other storefronts such as the Epic Games Store and GOG frequently run their own giveaways, giving budget-conscious gamers plenty of chances to expand their libraries without spending money.
