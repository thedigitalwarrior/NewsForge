Amazon has added a new Batman title to its Luna cloud gaming service, and it costs nothing to play. Batman: Caped Crusader Chronicles was released to coincide with the debut of season two of Amazon's animated Batman: Caped Crusader show, and it arrived without much fanfare.

## What the game offers

The game casts players as partners to Batman as they work through eleven episodic detective cases set in Gotham City. Familiar villains such as The Joker and Scarecrow appear across the cases. According to the developers, each episode includes detective-themed minigames designed for group play, with support for up to three players at once.

Unlike most games in the Batman catalog, Caped Crusader Chronicles doesn't require a console, a gaming PC, or even a controller. It runs through Amazon Luna, the company's cloud streaming platform, meaning it can be played on a smartphone. To join a session, at least one participant needs an active Amazon Prime membership and a phone capable of running the Luna app; the rest of the group can join in as guests.

## Part of Luna's GameNight lineup

The title is included in Luna's GameNight collection, a set of social party games meant to be played with friends in the same way one might use a service like Jackbox. Other games currently available for free through Luna include Dispatch, a narrative-driven superhero game built around player choices, Marvel's Guardians of the Galaxy, and entries from the Jackbox Party Pack series.

Amazon has acknowledged that a large share of Prime subscribers are unaware they already have access to Luna's game library as part of their membership. In response, the company recently introduced a dedicated Luna tab within the Prime Video section of its app, aiming to make these titles easier to find. Despite the change, the tab may still go unnoticed by many users, which is notable given that Luna's free offerings include more substantial titles like Fallout 4 and Alan Wake 2 alongside lighter party games such as Caped Crusader Chronicles.

## Tied to a new season of the animated show

The game's release lines up with the launch of the second season of Batman: Caped Crusader, a 1940s noir-inspired animated series that debuted its ten new episodes on July 31. Reviewers have described the new season as a mixed follow-up to the first, which was generally well received, noting that it only occasionally reaches the quality of Batman: The Animated Series, the classic 1990s show it draws inspiration from. Critics have singled out the performance of The Joker in the new season as a highlight, even as the broader season struggles to consistently match its predecessor.

For now, Caped Crusader Chronicles remains available to any Amazon Prime member willing to track down the Luna tab and gather a couple of friends for a round of Gotham-set detective work.
