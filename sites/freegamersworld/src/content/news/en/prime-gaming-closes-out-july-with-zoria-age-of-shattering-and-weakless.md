## Final July Freebies

Prime Gaming has released its last pair of free games for July, closing out the month's lineup with two titles claimable through Amazon's "Claim Games" section. Subscribers can add tactical RPG Zoria: Age of Shattering and cooperative puzzle adventure Weakless to their libraries, both distributed as GOG codes rather than through the Amazon Games app.

## Zoria: Age of Shattering

Zoria: Age of Shattering is described as the more valuable of the two offerings, since it currently retails for $11.24 on Steam and has never dropped below $6.85 in past sales. The game is a turn-based tactical RPG set in a large fantasy world, where players lead a squad through battles against hostile forces while also managing a home base between missions, balancing exploration, combat, and resource management.

## Weakless

The second free title, Weakless, is a narrative-driven puzzle adventure centered on two friends navigating an unfamiliar world together. One character is deaf and the other blind, and the pair must depend on each other's differing senses and abilities to solve puzzles and progress through obstacles they could not overcome alone. The game emphasizes cooperative problem-solving and its story of friendship built on complementary strengths.

## Claiming the Games

Access to both freebies requires an active Amazon Prime membership. Once subscribed, players can redeem the games directly on Amazon's website or through the direct GOG links provided in Prime Gaming's claim section. As with previous monthly batches, the games are added automatically once claimed and remain playable for as long as the Prime subscription stays active for DRM-linked titles, though GOG codes typically grant permanent ownership once redeemed.

New subscribers who have not yet used a Prime trial can access a 30-day free trial to claim these two games along with the rest of July's Prime Gaming catalog before it rotates out. Trial offers are available across several regions, including the United States, Germany, the United Kingdom, Spain, France, and Poland. In the US, Amazon also offers an extended six-month free trial for users aged 18 to 24. All trials convert to a paid subscription automatically unless cancelled before the trial period ends.

## Looking Ahead

With July's free games now fully revealed, attention turns to August's lineup, which Amazon is expected to announce the following Thursday. Prime Gaming has maintained a regular cadence of monthly free game reveals, typically split across two or more batches throughout the month, alongside in-game content drops for various live-service titles.
