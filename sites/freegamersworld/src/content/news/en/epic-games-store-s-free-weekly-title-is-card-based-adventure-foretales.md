Epic Games Store's latest weekly giveaway is Foretales, a story-driven card game developed by Alkemi. The title is free to claim from July 23 until July 30 at 11am ET, and like all Epic freebies, it stays in players' libraries permanently once claimed.

Unlike recent weeks, which featured double giveaways, this round offers only a single game. Epic has already confirmed what follows: OTXO and Sol Cesto will become available for free starting July 30 and running through August 6.

## What Foretales Is About

Foretales casts players as Volepain, a small-time thief and anthropomorphic shoebill, in a world threatened by a dark prophecy. According to the developer, a deity called the Weaver once entrusted her children, the Nymphants, with instruments capable of both creating and destroying the game's many worlds. With an apocalypse looming, players must decide how to spend the little time left, uncovering the true motives of the Nymphants and a mysterious cult along the way. The narration is provided by Travis Willingham, known for his work on Critical Role.

## Card-Based Adventure Mechanics

Rather than relying on traditional dialogue trees or turn-based combat, Foretales builds its adventure-game structure around cards. Players progress by combining cards and exploring decks, using skills and consumable items to interact with characters and situations they encounter. The game reportedly emphasizes flexibility in how conflicts are resolved: players can choose non-violent routes, such as playing a 'fame' card to intimidate an opponent into fleeing, or offering food to defuse a tense encounter, rather than defaulting to combat.

The game is built around branching storylines, with the developer promising multiple endings and a design that evokes the feel of a board game. Reviewers covering the title have noted its distinctive artwork and heavy narrative focus as it draws players deeper into its world.

## What's Coming Next

Once the Foretales promotion ends, Epic will offer two titles simultaneously starting July 30. OTXO is a fast-paced, top-down roguelite shooter in which an unnamed protagonist fights through a surreal mansion to rescue a lost love, combining bullet-time mechanics with a wide arsenal of guns and abilities. Sol Cesto is a tactical roguelite centered on risk management, tasking players with descending through a randomly generated dungeon, choosing tiles that may yield treasure, monsters, or traps, while using magical items and curses to influence their odds along the way.

As usual, players interested in claiming Foretales can do so through the Epic Games Store website or launcher before the offer closes.
