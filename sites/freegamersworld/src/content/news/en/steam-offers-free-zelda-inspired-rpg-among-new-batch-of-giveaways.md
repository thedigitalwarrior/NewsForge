Valve's storefront continues to expand its selection of no-cost titles, with a handful of small indie releases now available to claim and keep permanently, no payment or subscription required.

## A Link to the Past-inspired puzzle-adventure

The most notable of the new freebies is The Apprentice, developed by Quite a Coven. The game draws clear inspiration from The Legend of Zelda: A Link to the Past, the 1991 Super Nintendo classic, though it is not affiliated with Nintendo in any way. Players are cast as a character stranded in an alien world who must use rune-based magic to find a way home, combining spellcrafting, puzzle-solving, and exploration in a top-down format reminiscent of 1990s action-adventure games. On Steam, The Apprentice currently holds a "Positive" user rating based on a small pool of ten reviews, with early feedback describing it as a short but enjoyable puzzle experience.

## Vampire tale and Brazilian folklore RPG

Alongside The Apprentice, Steam users can also grab Dracula 2 from developer GNOMEPUNK, released earlier this month. The game positions itself as a sequel to Bram Stoker's novel, tasking players with helping the imprisoned Dracula escape, and has drawn comparisons to a mix of Vampire: The Masquerade - Bloodlines and Undertale in tone and presentation. It has likewise earned a "Positive" rating from its first ten user reviews.

A third addition, Estrada Real - Caminho das Almas, is a top-down RPG styled after Commodore 64-era games of the 1980s. Drawing on the folklore of Brazil's Minas Gerais region, it centers on corrupted souls and ancient magic. The title has not yet accumulated the minimum ten reviews needed for Steam to display a user rating score, but its retro aesthetic and cultural setting make it a distinctive entry among the recent free releases.

None of these three games carry an expiration date, meaning players can add them to their libraries at any time without risk of missing a limited-time window.

## More free titles added elsewhere on Steam

Separately, Steam's free catalog has also grown with additional titles spotted in recent days, including Last Lumen Knight, an action game built around intense combat against waves of enemies in a dark setting; Debris Zone, a survival and exploration game focused on resource management amid hostile alien structures; Tidy Up: Spaceship, a low-pressure cleaning and organization simulator set aboard a spacecraft; and Vexbound, a fast-paced action title built around combat and progression through escalating obstacles. As with the Zelda-inspired RPG and its companions, these games can be claimed for free and kept permanently once added to a Steam library.
