Steam's ongoing free-to-keep promotion has put Yet Another Zombie Defense HD (YAZDHD) up for grabs at no cost, but the offer expires soon. The game must be added to a Steam library before July 30 at 5 PM UTC (10 AM PT / 1 PM ET), after which it returns to its regular price of $3.99.

## What the game offers

Released in 2017 by Awesome Games Studio, YAZDHD is a top-down, twin-stick shooter that alternates between daytime base-building and nighttime survival against waves of zombies. Players spend daylight hours constructing barricades, placing turrets, and saving up currency to buy weapons and defensive upgrades, then defend their base once the undead swarm at night. Difficulty escalates with each successive night.

The game supports both local and online co-op for up to four players and includes three modes: Defense, Endless, and Deathmatch. It also features Steam Workshop support, letting players download community-made cosmetic skins for their survivors, including crossover-style designs referencing characters like Hatsune Miku, Garfield, Deadpool, and Shrek.

On Steam, the game currently holds an "Very Positive" rating, with reviewers noting its resemblance to older browser and indie zombie titles from the flash-game era, despite its 2017 release date.

## Why it's free now

According to PC Gamer, the giveaway coincides with the promotion of Yet Another Zombie Survivors, an upcoming bullet-heaven style game sharing the same visual style and escalating horde concept, featuring enemies such as giant werewolves and mummies. That title is currently in early access and is scheduled to reach its full 1.0 release on August 20.

## How to claim it

Claiming the game requires nothing more than visiting its Steam store page and clicking "Add to Account" before the deadline. Once added, it remains permanently in the user's library, regardless of future price changes.

## More free games on the way

Steam's free-to-keep promotion, which periodically offers full games at no charge for a limited window, is set to continue into August, with Moonlighter and Deponia already confirmed as upcoming additions, according to GameRant. Exact dates for those releases have not yet been announced. Players interested in tracking future giveaways are advised to check Steam's promotional page regularly, as such offers tend to run for only a short period before reverting to standard pricing.
