## Two Roguelikes Available at No Cost

Epic Games has refreshed its weekly PC giveaway, replacing last week's Foretales with a pair of well-regarded roguelike titles: Sol Cesto and OTXO. Both games can be claimed for free through the Epic Games Store until August 6, 2026, at 11 a.m. EDT, after which Beacon Pines and We Were Here Together are set to take their place.

## Sol Cesto: Chance Meets Strategy

Sol Cesto sends players into a dungeon in search of a lost sun, descending through a series of levels structured as 4x4 grids where each tile might hide an enemy, treasure, or hazard. Rather than moving freely, players must weigh the risk of each square as they progress, gradually growing stronger through relics, magical items, and curses that shape each run.
