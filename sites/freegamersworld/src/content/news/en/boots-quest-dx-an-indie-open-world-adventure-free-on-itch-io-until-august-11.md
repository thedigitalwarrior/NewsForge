PC players have a limited window to add Boots Quest DX to their libraries at no cost. The hand-drawn open-world adventure, normally priced around $10, is currently available as a free claim-and-keep title on itch.io, with the offer set to expire on August 11.

## About the Game

Boots Quest DX was created by solo developer Harrison Lemke and released in 2022. Since then, it has cultivated a small but loyal following drawn to its offbeat tone and unconventional storytelling. The developer describes it as "a low-key, offbeat adventure about traveling to the ends of the earth in search of the Best Boots."

The game centers on a simple premise: players guide the protagonist across a compact but open hand-drawn world in search of the perfect pair of boots. Along the journey, players meet a cast of characters, tackle side quests, and uncover secrets scattered throughout the map. Rather than emphasizing challenge or puzzle-solving typical of many 2D adventure titles, Boots Quest DX leans into a relaxed, low-pressure pace paired with a bittersweet, melancholic atmosphere.

The soundtrack has been highlighted by fans and reviewers as one of the game's strongest features. Lemke composed the music himself, with contributions from additional musicians, resulting in what one Steam reviewer called "warm, saturated 4-track psychedelia tunes" that reinforce the game's nostalgic mood.

## Reception and Technical Details

On Steam, Boots Quest DX holds a 100% positive rating, though the sample size remains modest at just over 60 user reviews. While that number is too small to draw sweeping conclusions, the unanimous positivity suggests the game resonates strongly with the niche audience it has attracted.

The game is also notably lightweight from a technical standpoint. Its base download is only about 65 MB, and its system requirements are minimal enough to run on hardware that is a decade or more old, making it accessible to players without high-end setups.

## Claiming the Game

To receive Boots Quest DX for free, PC users need an itch.io account and must claim the title before the August 11 deadline. Once claimed, the game remains permanently in the user's library at no additional cost.

Boots Quest DX is one of several free offerings currently circulating for PC gamers in August 2026. On Steam, the roguelike Dome: Become Thyself is available at no cost before it converts to a paid release, while Mystic Depths: The Corrupted King, previously priced at $11.99, has also been made free to keep.
