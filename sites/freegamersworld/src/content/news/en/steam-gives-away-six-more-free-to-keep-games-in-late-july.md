Steam continues its recent run of no-strings-attached giveaways with a new set of six titles that players can add to their libraries and keep permanently, no time limit attached. Unlike the limited free-weekend promotions that require players to grab a game within 24 hours, this batch is a straightforward permanent giveaway, according to a report from ScreenRant.

## Visual Novels Lead the Pack

Two of the six games are visual novels aimed at players who want a shorter, story-driven experience. Tyrania Enforcers is a hand-drawn visual novel with several minigames and a runtime estimated at three to five hours. It follows a journalist named Linda as she accompanies enforcers of the fictional Tyranian Empire and begins to question the cost of the order they maintain. User reviews cited by ScreenRant praise its animation and minigames, and note that it runs comfortably on low-end hardware.

The second, Cosdarica's Journey at the End of the World, is a much shorter experience, clocking in at under an hour according to its own developers, with a single ending, three original songs, and ten illustrated scenes. It's set in a post-apocalyptic world where most survivors have given up and retreated into shelters to await death, except for the title character. Reviewers described it as brief but memorable, with world-building that feels more developed than its short length would suggest.

## Horror and Simulation Entries

SSTWL - Sector Breach is a point-and-click survival horror game inspired by the YouTube series "Scary Story Time With Liam." It borrows heavily from the Five Nights at Freddy's formula, having players monitor security cameras, close doors, and fend off monsters over roughly six hours of gameplay. According to user reviews referenced in the report, the game distinguishes itself from the flood of low-effort FNAF-style clones commonly found on itch.io and other platforms.

Road Diner Simulator: Prologue offers a free preview of the upcoming full game, letting players run a roadside diner on Route 66 before its eventual rebuild depicted in the main release. The prologue lets players manage the business for a few in-game days.

## Idle RPG and More

Lost Vessel rounds out the notable entries as a sci-fi idle RPG built around real-time combat and crafting, designed to be played either in short thirty-second sessions or extended multi-hour sittings. The game is entirely free with no microtransactions, according to its developers, and reviewers note that its crafting and resource systems reward both casual check-ins and longer play sessions, including a chance at crafting rare "legendary" items.

## Availability

All six games are currently listed on Steam with no cost and no expiration date for claiming them, distinguishing this giveaway from earlier limited-time free weekends for titles such as Metal Gear Solid, Icarus, and Killing Floor 3. Interested players can find each title through its respective Steam store page and add it to their library at no charge.
