If you play on PC, you can build a sizeable library without spending anything — as long as you know where to look and, crucially, when to claim. Four storefronts give games away on a regular basis, and each works a little differently. Here is how to keep up with all of them.

## Epic Games Store: a new free game every week

The Epic Games Store is the most predictable of the bunch. Every week it gives away at least one PC game, and the offer rotates on a fixed schedule. Once you claim a title, it is yours to keep permanently, even if you never install it. The only requirement is a free Epic account. Because the window is short — typically a week — the habit that pays off is checking in regularly rather than waiting for a specific title.

## Steam: free to keep, but watch the deadline

Steam handles free games differently. Rather than a dedicated weekly slot, publishers occasionally make a game **free to keep** for a limited time, often to promote a sequel or a major update. Claim it during the promotion and it stays in your library forever; miss the window and it reverts to its normal price. Steam also lists permanently free-to-play titles, which are a separate category — those are always available and do not expire.

## GOG: DRM-free giveaways

GOG, run by the studio behind The Witcher, periodically offers a game for free, often tied to a sale or an anniversary. GOG's distinguishing feature is that its games are **DRM-free**: once downloaded, the installer is yours with no launcher required. As with the others, free GOG games are usually available for a limited time, so the claim deadline matters.

## Prime Gaming: monthly drops for Prime members

Prime Gaming bundles a rotating set of free games with an Amazon Prime subscription. Members get several titles each month, some redeemable on Amazon's own client and others as codes for stores like GOG or Epic. Unlike the other three, this one is not free in the strict sense — it is a perk of a paid Prime membership — but if you already subscribe, it is worth claiming the monthly batch.

## The one rule that ties them together

Across all four stores, the pattern is the same: **the game is free, but the window to claim it is not open forever.** Claiming a title adds it to your account permanently; forgetting to claim before the deadline means paying full price later.
