Steam users have a limited window to add the free-to-play action title Arena of Trophies to their permanent library before the game disappears from the platform entirely. Developer Thor Game Studios has confirmed the 2024 hack-and-slash game will be delisted on October 22, 2026, giving players roughly three months from the announcement to secure the game.

## What Happens When a Game Is Delisted

Once Arena of Trophies is pulled from Steam's storefront, new users will have no way to acquire it. However, Steam's system allows anyone who has already added a title to their account library to keep access to it indefinitely, even after removal. That means players don't need to download or install the game right away; simply clicking "Add to Library" on the store page before the October cutoff is enough to guarantee future access.

Thor Game Studios has stated that Arena of Trophies does not depend on external servers or online services and includes no in-game purchases, which should allow those who claim it to keep playing normally after the delisting date.

## About Arena of Trophies

Arena of Trophies is a gladiator-themed action game in which players select from fifteen different combatants and fight through arena battles. According to the game's own description, the goal is to "collect achievements, refine your strategies, and become a legend in the endless fight for survival and recognition." Combat encounters incorporate environmental traps scattered around the arenas, giving players tactical options beyond straightforward fighting.

Unlike many Steam giveaways tied to promotional events, Arena of Trophies has always been free to play. Its removal from the storefront doesn't change its pricing model — it simply ends the ability for new players to discover and claim it through Steam.

## Why It's Being Delisted

According to the developer, the decision to delist stems from an inability to continue dedicating the time and resources needed to properly maintain, update, test, and support the game going forward. Thor Game Studios also indicated it does not want new players picking up the title under those unsupported conditions, which appears to be the primary motivation behind removing it from sale rather than leaving it available in a neglected state.

Small and indie titles disappearing from Steam is not an uncommon occurrence, as maintaining a live storefront presence requires ongoing developer commitment. Ultimately, the decision to delist rests with the studio behind the game.

## Other Free Offers on Steam

Arena of Trophies' looming departure comes as Steam currently hosts several other free offerings. The platform's Free to Keep promotion includes the zombie survival shooter Yet Another Zombie Defense HD at no cost, while a rotating Free Weekend selection currently features six additional titles available to try temporarily. Players looking to expand their libraries without spending money have a number of options to explore alongside Arena of Trophies before its October removal.
