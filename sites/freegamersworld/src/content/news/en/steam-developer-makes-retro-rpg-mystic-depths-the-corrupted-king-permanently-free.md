Solo developer François Duchesne has made his indie RPG Mystic Depths: The Corrupted King completely free on Steam, removing the $11.99 price tag the title carried since its release just weeks earlier. The game had already seen a price cut in June, but as of July 23 it appears to be permanently available at no cost rather than through a limited-time discount, meaning anyone who claims it can add it to their library and keep it indefinitely.

## A Retro-Inspired RPG

Mystic Depths: The Corrupted King launched on June 23, 2026, and draws visual comparisons to classic Japanese role-playing games, particularly early Final Fantasy entries such as Final Fantasy 4, thanks to its top-down pixel art style and medieval fantasy setting. The single-player title emphasizes exploration-driven level design, turn-based combat, and a build-crafting system built around permanent character buffs. A dynamic day-and-night cycle also influences gameplay, and the developer estimates roughly 30 hours of content for players who complete the story.

While the presentation nods to genre classics, the combat and progression systems are described as considerably simpler than those found in mainline Final Fantasy games, reflecting its scope as a solo development effort. According to the listed system requirements, the game is lightweight, running on integrated graphics with just 4GB of RAM, and the developer notes it should work on most laptops made within the last decade.

## Part of a Wider Trend of Free Steam Titles

Duchesne's decision adds to a pattern of small studios opting to give away recently released games rather than continue selling them, a move that can help build a player base or generate visibility for a developer's other work. Steam users have separately been able to claim the gladiator combat game Arena of Trophies for free before it is delisted from the storefront in October, after which it will no longer be available to new players who missed the offer.

Looking ahead, free-to-keep offerings on Steam have reportedly been sparse this summer. According to SteamDB tracking data, the cozy roguelike RPG Moonlighter is expected to become free to keep sometime in August, though it remains the only confirmed giveaway on the platform for that month so far.

By comparison, the Epic Games Store has kept a steadier cadence of giveaways, currently offering the narrative card game Foretales, with OTXO and Sol Cesto scheduled to become free on July 30. Whether Steam adds further free titles beyond Moonlighter before August ends remains to be seen.
